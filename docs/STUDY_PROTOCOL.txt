MACHINE LEARNING-BASED ANALYSIS AND PREDICTION OF ANTIBIOTIC RESISTANCE
Prime study: preprocessing of Salmonella phenotype and genomic-annotation data
Protocol written before model results were inspected, 18 October 2026 (Asia/Dhaka).

1. QUESTION
How do categorical encoding and numerical missing-value strategies affect a
genomic AMR classifier after auditable, biologically informed cleaning? What
data are lost or changed by the cleaning, and how reproducible/scalable is it?

2. DATA AND UNIT OF ANALYSIS
Acquire the full NCBI Pathogen Detection organism-group query for Salmonella
enterica AST, and all matching tested-isolate metadata. Confirm species_taxid
28901. Archive responses, stable order, timestamps, schema, and SHA256. Counts
of AST rows, BioSamples, assemblies, and SNP groups are distinct denominators.
This is a public surveillance/research convenience cohort. No population
resistance prevalence inference, patient-level study, or raw-genome discovery
claim is supported. Reference annotations are retrospective and curated.

3. DETERMINISTIC CLEANING
Preserve raw files. Normalize only Unicode/whitespace/case and '=' equality.
IDs remain strings. Record every modified cell. Check submission-domain MIC
and disk measurements, preserving signs and combination concentrations.
Never replace censored >x with an exact measurement of x. Keep MIC mg/L and
disk mm separate. Source labels are retained; no current breakpoint relabeling.
Quarantine quantitative measurement anomalies for quantitative use; they are
not genotype/phenotype discrepancies. Do not remove genomic discordance.

Deduplicate identical biological assay payloads; distinguish intentional
long-format multiple drugs and repeat assays. Resolve no conflicts by vote:
all discordant phenotype pairs are quarantined. Missing and nonbinary targets
are never imputed. Primary R/S pairs require unanimous known labels and an
unambiguous metadata join. Quarantine nonunique BioSample metadata. Audit
both raw target-accession mismatches and unmatched BioSamples.

Dates are interval/precision-aware. Only strict year, month, day and explicit
ranges are accepted. Do not invent days or use ingestion date as collection.
Assembly metrics must be positive and N50 cannot exceed total assembly size.
There is no arbitrary species-genome-length trimming based on the outcome.

4. GENOMIC ENCODING
AMR_genotypes is a unique symbol list, not a gene-copy counter. Split only the
final '=': preserve symbol punctuation, point mutations, and call status.
Use train-fitted multi-hot encoding. A completed analysis with explicit zero
reported calls is distinct from unavailable results. The raw API can omit an
empty array: availability therefore uses applied flag plus a reported list or
an explicit zero count. Positive count plus missing list is unavailable.
The inclusive AMR list can contain general efflux/plus calls. Do not call all
tokens acquired resistance genes. Provide gene-only and metadata-only controls.
Unknown validation/test tokens get no fitted column; count and report them.

5. PREDECLARED TASKS AND PREDICTORS
Primary drug: ampicillin. Secondary: tetracycline and ciprofloxacin, provided
each has at least 100 eligible R and 100 S BioSamples. Secondary outcomes do
not select the primary result. R=1, S=0. Intermediate, nonsusceptible and other
labels stay in the cleaned data but outside this binary estimand.

Candidate X: country, host, source/epidemiological type, serovar, collection
year, assembly N50/length/contigs, unique AMR call count, availability flag,
and reported genomic symbols. Never use MIC, disk, susceptibility, assay-panel
statistics, number_drugs_*, AST_phenotypes, sample/accession/project/cluster IDs
as X. This is prediction after genomic processing, not before culture growth.

6. SPLITTING AND COMPARISONS
Build connected groups linked by BioSample and recorded SNP cluster. Use fixed
random seed42 and 60/20/20 training/validation/test group allocation. Missing
clusters fall back to BioSample; do not group all missing clusters together.
These groups reduce known clone overlap but cannot prove phylogenetic or
patient independence. Split once before all learned preprocessing.

Use fixed logistic regression C=1 and no class weighting. Compare ordinal
integer vs one-hot nominal encoding crossed with (a) median, (b) median plus
missingness indicators, (c) multivariate iterative imputation plus indicators.
Fit encoders/imputers/scalers/vocabulary within training folds. Integer nominal
codes are a deliberate ablation and imply an artificial order for a linear
model. IterativeImputer is deterministic multivariate imputation, not a full
multiple-imputation inferential analysis. It is not assumed superior.

Primary configuration fixed in advance: one-hot plus median and indicators,
with genomic multi-hot features. Primary threshold0.5. A validation-only
max-F1 threshold is supplementary. Three-fold grouped CV within training is
the internal comparison. All six frozen comparisons may be reported on the
same holdout as a prespecified analysis, without test-guided tuning. Report
metadata-only and genomic-only controls and additional split sensitivity.

7. MEASURES AND UNCERTAINTY
Report average precision (primary), AUROC, resistance recall/precision/F1,
balanced accuracy, accuracy, Brier score, calibration and confusion matrices,
alongside class counts. Use paired resampling of entire held-out groups for
confidence intervals/differences; include effective groups, not just rows.
Confidence intervals are conditional on this fitted model and cohort.
Single-center/source/lineage biases and uncertain clusterless relationships
remain. No model is approved for clinical use by this experiment.

8. COMPUTATIONAL EVALUATION AND REPRODUCTION
Benchmark real prefixes and full acquired data with several chunk sizes.
Verify identical canonical hashes across chunk sizes. Do not manufacture
millions of rows or claim a distributed benchmark. Record measured time,
peak process memory, versions, actual run logs, and inference round-trip.
Deliver WSL scripts, frozen data, audits, executable notebook, saved models,
results and an explanatory report. Include AI assistance disclosure. This
protocol supports an undergraduate reproducible empirical study; it does not
prejudge publication novelty or promise favorable metrics.
